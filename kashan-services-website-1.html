FAQ

Questions People Usually Ask

Do I need Power BI installed to use my dashboard? +

Not necessarily. I can publish your dashboard to Power BI Service (the web version), which means anyone with a link can view it in a browser — no software needed. If you need the full editable file, I'll walk you through how to open it.

What format should my data be in? +

Any format works — Excel, CSV, Google Sheets, a database, or even a PDF table. If your data is messy or spread across multiple files, that's fine too. Data cleaning is part of the process.

How long does a dashboard take to build? +

Most dashboards are delivered within 3 to 7 business days, depending on complexity and how many data sources are involved. I'll give you a clear timeline in the proposal before we start.

Can I update the dashboard myself after it's built? +

Yes. I build dashboards that auto-refresh when you update the underlying data. I'll also do a handover walkthrough so you know exactly how to maintain and update it — no technical knowledge needed.

Do you work with students and researchers? +

Absolutely — students and researchers are a big part of who I work with. I help with thesis data analysis using SPSS or R, statistical interpretation, and presenting findings in a clear, structured way your supervisor will approve.

How do I pay, and is there a refund policy? +

I work with a 50% upfront payment and 50% on delivery. If you're not satisfied with the final result, I offer revisions until it meets the agreed scope. I want you to be genuinely happy with the work.
